# House style for figures

Paste this ahead of every figure brief.

- **Look:** hand-drawn comic explainer. Warm off-white paper background, thick dark-ink rounded outlines, soft pastel fills (blue, lilac, mint, peach, rose, butter yellow), a gentle drop shadow on boxes. Hand-lettered, highly legible text.
- **Recurring characters** (match the two reference strips, `src/images/strip-1-python-actors.png` and `src/images/strip-2-who-owns-the-thread.png`):
  - **Rust:** a smiling orange cog with eyes.
  - **Python:** a friendly blue-and-yellow snake.
  - **Your actor:** a small lilac blob with a smiley face and crossed arms.
- **Wires:** thick dark lines with arrowheads. The state wire is always drawn heavier than the others and runs horizontally.
- **Math:** set in a clean serif italic, like LaTeX. Use the exact symbols given; do not paraphrase them.
- **Canvas:** 1600 × 700 px, landscape, generous margins, no title bar (the article provides the caption).
- **Text:** only the labels listed in the brief. No extra words.
